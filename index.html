HOW IT WORKS

Three little steps. A lot of local magic.

1. Find your treat. Open your personal booklet to see the offers waiting for your family.

2. Visit somewhere lovely. Browse the participating Hexham businesses and use the directions link to find each one.

3. Claim it when you're there. Tap Use this treat only when you're ready at the business, then show the QR code to the staff member.

Each booklet is personal. A treat is reserved for 10 minutes after you choose to use it, so please wait until you're at the business.
